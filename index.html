Fire Wall.

GNF is the FIREWALL floor-stabilizer protocol on Metaplex Core, live on Solana mainnet. This is where under-floor listings are challenged. Frozen NFTs enter a 6 hour timed window: owners can defrost before expiry, or holders vote to save or burn. Every outcome is tracked here, from live votes to final results and your own voting history.

How FIREWALL Works (The 9-Step Loop)

1 Detect Under-floor listing detected in real time.
2 Freeze The NFT is frozen automatically.
3 Window The owner has 6 hours to resolve.
4 Defreeze Marketplace API delists it; owner keeps asset.
5 Vote If unresolved, 12-hour holder vote starts.
6 Burn If burned, original NFT is destroyed.
7 Rebirth New NFT (same traits) minted to loyal holders.
8 Save If vote fails, NFT is released normally.
9 Loop Repeated undercuts re-trigger automatically.

Live Burn or Save votes appear here. Review each frozen NFT, check the timer, and cast your vote before the window closes.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about GNF, FIREWALL, and the ecosystem.

What is GNF?

GNF — Government Not Found — is a Web3 ecosystem built around FIREWALL, an automated on-chain protocol that defends NFT floor prices. Built on Metaplex Core, live on Solana mainnet, pre-launch on the social side.

What is FIREWALL in one sentence?

A collection-level protocol that freezes any NFT listed below the community floor, gives the seller 6 hours to back down, then lets holders vote to burn it — with burned assets re-minted and redistributed to holders who defended the project.

Is GNF live?

The protocol is on Solana mainnet. The social and pack rollout has not started. We're in a pre-launch phase, raising an angel round.

Is the GNF token live?

No. The token is intentionally not active. It will be introduced after FIREWALL proves real adoption. There are no current token promises, no presale, no allocation commitments.

Who decides the floor?

The community. The team has no override.

What stops someone from gaming the vote?

The vote is on-chain, scoped to holders, and time-bound. The protocol has anti-manipulation logic: repeated under-floor relistings re-trigger enforcement automatically.

How does GNF make money?

FIREWALL is sold as subscription infrastructure. Tiers: Starter $50/mo (1 collection), Pro $100/mo (2), Advanced $150/mo (3), Enterprise $300/mo (unlimited). Yearly is 20% off. NFT holders get 50% off. 20% of revenue funds GNF token buybacks.

How do I get a GNF NFT?

By participating. You earn leader-specific fragments through social tasks, referrals, and Enlightenment Stories. 100 fragments → 1 Leader pack. Once all 10,000 packs are minted across the community, claiming opens and packs deploy NFTs on-chain. Total supply is capped at 10,000.

Can I buy a pack directly?

Yes — but only a limited share. 2,000 NFTs across the whole collection are available for direct purchase from the Store using burn coins, and 8,000 must be earned (sacrifice-minted from fragments, granted as rewards from tasks/raffles/boxes, or seeded).

What are the four Leaders?

Owl, Wolf, Shark, Snake. Each Leader has a dedicated pack type and fragment type. Choosing a Leader path is part of the user identity in GNF.

What happens to a burned NFT?

Its metadata and traits are preserved. A reborn NFT with the same traits is minted and redistributed to holders via a community reward mechanism — never returned to the original under-floor lister.

Can the team override FIREWALL?

No. Once deployed, FIREWALL operates without manual intervention. There are no team backdoors.

Is FIREWALL censorship?

No. FIREWALL is not a centralized actor freezing your asset. The rules are encoded into the asset configuration when the collection is enrolled — every holder consents to them by holding the asset. It's the contract of the asset, the same way royalty plugins or transfer restrictions work in any modern NFT standard.
